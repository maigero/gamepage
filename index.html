About the Project

Tales Beneath The Veil is an experimental puzzle adventure that combines classic escape-room mechanics with modern AI.

The starting point was the prototype EscapeLLM, in which we first tested how a large language model can be integrated directly into a game. The goal was not just to generate dialogue, but to dynamically influence the game itself.

In the current version, this concept has been further developed: the AI is more deeply embedded in the gameplay, reacts more flexibly, and enables a significantly more immersive gaming experience.

Core Idea

The player talks directly with an NPC who can give hints, deceive, or withhold information. This creates an interaction that feels less like a classic game and more like a real conversation.

Gameplay Experience

The game leads through several rooms with different puzzles – from classic search tasks to creative challenges. The NPC accompanies the player throughout and becomes the central element.

Result

The project shows how AI can be used deliberately to enable new forms of gameplay: more dynamic, more personal, and less predictable.

Highlights
